How to load a real ticker
  • Free, 15-min delayed (CBOE): open https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/delayed_quotes/options/SPY.json in your browser (swap SPY for your ticker; indexes use an underscore, e.g. _SPX), save the page as a .json file, then drop it here. It already includes open interest, IV, gamma and the spot price.
  • Any CSV export (broker, Barchart, ThinkorSwim, etc.) with one row per contract and columns for strike, expiration, type (C/P), open_interest, and either gamma or iv. An OCC symbol column can stand in for strike/expiry/type. Set the spot price above.

Per contract: GEX = gamma × open interest × 100 × spot² × 1%. Calls count positive, puts negative. When a file has no gamma, it is computed with Black‑Scholes from IV (r = 4%). Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
